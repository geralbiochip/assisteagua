Services

Diagnosis and repair

We assess ignition failures, unexpected shutdowns and water heating problems. Work is defined after examining the equipment and reported symptoms.

Ignition and components

Depending on the model, we check ignition components, valves, sensors, electronic controls and the heat exchanger. Repair or replacement depends on diagnosis, compatibility and parts availability.

Temperature and flow

We assess operation and supply conditions when water is cold, excessively hot or unstable during use. Adjustments and replacement are proposed after diagnosis.

Installation and replacement

We help select equipment according to consumption and installation conditions, including gas supply and exhaust. Removal of old equipment, adaptations, checks and any applicable procedures must appear in the quotation.

Maintenance and cleaning

Work is adapted to the model and manufacturer instructions. It may include cleaning, combustion assessment and checks of connections, leaks, components and safety devices.

Descaling and gas compatibility

We assess heat exchanger descaling needs and compatibility of procedures. Any conversion to another gas type requires prior assessment of the model and manufacturer instructions; it is not advertised as possible for all equipment.

Gas smell or suspected leak

Do not use a flame to locate a leak or attempt repairs. Do not smoke, operate switches or turn electrical appliances on or off; do not use a phone in the affected area. If safe, shut off the gas supply and open doors and windows. Leave the area and, from outside, contact your gas network operator or supplier emergency line, or the fire service.

AssisteÁgua’s business contact and website form are not emergency services. Do not wait for a form response if a gas leak is suspected.

Common problems

The same symptom may have different causes. These topics help describe a request but do not replace diagnosis or provide repair instructions.

The water heater does not ignite

We assess ignition, the supply applicable to the model, sensors, controls and water and gas conditions. For battery-powered ignition, the battery supply is part of assessment.

The heater switches off during use

We check symptoms, sensors and safety devices, operating conditions and exhaust. Shutdown may result from protective action; the cause must be identified before proposing repair.

The flame is weak, irregular or unusual

Changes in flame or combustion require technical assessment. We examine the burner and applicable supply and exhaust conditions without determining the cause from flame appearance alone.

Water is cold, too hot or unstable

We assess temperature control, flow, regulating components and the heat exchanger. Adjustments, cleaning or replacement may be proposed according to diagnosis.

There are unusual noises

We assess operation, deposits in the heat exchanger, fixings and components, including the fan where fitted. Cleaning or replacement is proposed after identifying the noise source.

There are water leaks

We locate the source at connections, seals, the heat exchanger or other applicable components. We explain repair feasibility and alternatives without promising to restore damaged parts before assessment.

The display shows errors or electronic faults occur

Provide brand, model and any code exactly as displayed. Interpretation depends on the equipment and guides assessment without establishing the replacement part on its own.
