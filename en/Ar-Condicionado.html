Frequently asked questions

The unit does not start

Provide brand, model and any remote-control or display signals. We assess operation and relevant components before proposing repair.

Cooling or heating is below expectations

We assess use, equipment condition and suitability for the space. Reduced performance alone does not establish the required component or work.

Water is dripping or moisture appears

The source requires on-site assessment of mounting and operating conditions. Work is defined after identifying the fault.

There are noises, odours or unexpected stops

Describe when symptoms occur and whether one or several units are affected. We assess the system before recommending cleaning, adjustments or repair.

The display shows an error code

Send the code exactly as displayed, with brand and model. Interpretation depends on the equipment and does not replace diagnosis.
