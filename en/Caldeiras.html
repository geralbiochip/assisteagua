Multi-brand assistance for gas and oil boilers. We assess operating faults, heating and hot water problems and propose work appropriate to the equipment and installation conditions.

Services

Diagnosis and repair

We assess ignition failures, insufficient heating, unexpected shutdowns and error codes. Work is defined after examining the equipment and reported symptoms.

Pressure, leaks and circulation

According to the symptoms, we check the heating circuit, connections, valves, expansion vessel and water circulation. Proposed work depends on the source of the problem.

Component replacement

We assess circulation pumps, valves, sensors, thermostats, expansion vessels, heat exchangers and electronic components. Repair or replacement depends on diagnosis, compatibility and parts availability.

Installation and replacement

We help select a boiler suited to usage needs and the existing installation. Removal of old equipment, adaptations and planned checks must be included in the quotation, along with any applicable procedures or documents.

Preventive maintenance

Work is adapted to the boiler type and manufacturer instructions. It may include cleaning, combustion assessment and checks of components, leaks, pressure and safety devices.

Cleaning and descaling

We assess cleaning needs for the burner, heat exchanger and circuits covered by the work. Descaling, where appropriate, follows procedures suited to the materials and manufacturer instructions.

Gas and oil boilers

Gas boilers

Depending on the model and assessment, maintenance may cover the burner and heat-transfer components, combustion analysis, gas circuit, combustion-product evacuation, sensors and safety devices.

Oil boilers

Depending on the model and condition, work may include cleaning the boiler body and turbulators, assessing the burner, nozzle and fuel supply, and checking the exhaust circuit and safety components.

Common problems

The same symptom may have different causes. This information helps describe your request but does not replace diagnosis or provide repair instructions. Open the relevant topic.

The boiler does not heat water or radiators

We assess ignition, water circulation, temperature controls and the heating circuit. The issue may involve the pump, valves, sensors, room thermostat or other components; a symptom alone cannot confirm the cause.

Pressure is low or drops frequently

We check possible water losses and the operation of valves, the expansion vessel and other relevant components. The solution depends on the source of pressure loss and installation conditions.

The boiler makes unusual noises

Banging, whistling or vibration requires assessment. We examine circulation, air in the circuit, deposits, fixings and components before proposing cleaning, adjustments or parts replacement.

The boiler switches off by itself

We assess error records, sensors and safety devices, the supply and control components. Shutdown may result from protective action or another fault requiring diagnosis.

There are water leaks

We locate the source at connections, seals, valves, the heat exchanger or other components, depending on the equipment. We explain repair feasibility and alternatives. Restoration of damaged components is not promised without assessment.

The display shows an error code

Provide the brand, model and code exactly as displayed. Codes vary between manufacturers and models and guide assessment, but do not necessarily identify the part to replace on their own.

There are changes in flame or combustion

Changes in burner operation, ignition or combustion require suitable technical assessment. We check the components and operating conditions applicable to the model, including supply and exhaust where covered by the intervention.

The display goes blank or electrical faults occur

We assess supply, connections, controls and relevant electronic components. Whether a board or another part can be repaired or replaced is confirmed after diagnosis, without an advance repair guarantee.
