About us

More than 20 years of experience supporting your home’s comfort.

AssisteÁgua specialises in the installation, maintenance and repair of heating, hot water and air conditioning equipment. We combine technical experience with personal support, paying attention to each customer and installation.

We value clear communication, careful workmanship and respect for your space. Our aim is to help you understand the available options and find an appropriate response to each situation.

Technical team and specialist training

Practical experience is supported by technical qualifications and training at Instituto Tecnológico do Gás (ITG), in the areas reported by our team.

Gas technician

Technical qualification obtained at Instituto Tecnológico do Gás (ITG).

Gas appliance mechanic

Qualification in gas appliances obtained at ITG.

Gas network design

Gas design course completed at Instituto Tecnológico do Gás.

Each request is assessed against the equipment, intended work and applicable professional qualifications.

Experience and professionalism

AssisteÁgua repairs, maintains and installs heating, hot water and air conditioning equipment. With over 20 years of market experience, our specialised technicians assess each customer’s needs and propose appropriate solutions. We support new installations, equipment replacement and renovation of existing systems with care and attention to safety.

Our commitment

We listen to customers, assess the equipment carefully, explain proposed work and quotation conditions, and work professionally with attention to detail and respect for your space. We invest in updating our technicians’ knowledge as equipment evolves. Your satisfaction motivates us to improve how we handle each request.
