Services

Needs assessment

We examine intended use, existing equipment and site conditions. The solution follows confirmation of system type and scope.

Equipment selection

We help select options suited to the property and budget. Models, components and availability are confirmed in the proposal.

Installation and replacement

We assess mounting, connections and integration with covered equipment. Adaptations and old equipment removal must be expressly quoted.

System maintenance

Maintenance follows system type, model and manufacturer instructions. Intervals are assessed for the specific equipment.

Diagnosis and repair

We assess irregular performance, error signals and faults in covered components. Repair depends on diagnosis and parts availability.

Existing system renewal

We assess equipment condition and whether components can be retained, repaired or replaced. We explain options and work without promising savings.

Frequently asked questions

I want a new system

Provide location, property type and intended use. Assessment confirms equipment, installation conditions and included work.

Performance has decreased

Describe the change and when it started. We assess the system and use before attributing the cause to a component.

There are leaks, errors or irregular operation

Provide brand, model, system type and symptoms. Assistance and necessary work are confirmed after review.

Should I repair or replace it?

We assess system condition, parts availability and necessary work. Alternatives and prices are presented for your decision.

Is my system covered?

Specify whether the system is solar thermal or photovoltaic and the work requested. A general reference to solar panels does not by itself confirm assistance for all technologies or components.

Service scope

CAP SOLAR N.º 09/SOL/12537 is the responsible person’s individual reference, as supplied. It is not presented as general company authorisation for all solar technologies or work. Scope and applicable qualifications are confirmed before contracting.

Request assessment

Provide location, equipment or installation and the reason for your request. We confirm availability, appointments and costs, including travel and any diagnosis fee. Work and price are presented for acceptance.
