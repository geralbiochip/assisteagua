Services

Installation assessment

We examine the property, gas type and equipment to define the scope of work.

Indoor and outdoor networks

We assess installation, alteration or repair needs. The solution and applicable procedures are explained before acceptance.

Shared installations

We help condominium associations prepare interventions and coordinate work according to installation conditions.

Gas appliances and exhaust

We assess appliance fitting and assistance, extraction and exhaust systems within the confirmed intervention scope.

Pre-inspection and support

We help prepare for inspection and liaise with inspection bodies where applicable. This support does not replace any required inspection.

Design and procedures

We explain any design, inspection or documentation requirements and planned support. Responsible parties and included services are identified in the proposal.

Frequently asked questions

I want to install or alter a network

Provide location, property type, planned appliances and any existing installation. We assess work and procedures before quoting.

The building needs work

Send a description and any available reports. Scope, scheduling and coordination are confirmed after review.

An inspection identified faults

The report helps assess corrective work. Repairs and any later inspection are separate stages, with scope and costs to clarify.

I want to replace a gas appliance

Provide existing and proposed appliance brand and model. We assess installation compatibility and applicable procedures.

Service scope

Availability and scope depend on request analysis and applicable professional and business qualifications. No certificate issuing or inspections by AssisteÁgua are advertised without confirmation. The form and business contact are not emergency services.

Request assessment

Provide location, equipment or installation and the reason for your request. We confirm availability, appointments and costs, including travel and any diagnosis fee. Work and price are presented for acceptance.
